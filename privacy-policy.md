# HaloHueListingAssistant Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: October 11, 2026**

HaloHueListingAssistant is a private application operated by **Halo and Hue LLC** to assist with preparing and managing eBay product listings.

## Information Accessed

With authorization from the eBay account owner, HaloHueListingAssistant may access eBay inventory and listing information necessary to create and manage product listings and listing drafts.

## Information Storage

HaloHueListingAssistant is designed to minimize the collection and storage of eBay data. The application does not maintain a separate database of eBay buyer information or sales transaction records.

Authentication credentials required to communicate with eBay services will be handled securely and will not be publicly disclosed.

## Information Sharing

Halo and Hue LLC does not sell eBay account information or personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate HaloHueListingAssistant.

## Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect authentication credentials and information accessed by the application.

## Data Retention

Information is retained only as necessary to provide the application's functionality or meet applicable legal obligations.
